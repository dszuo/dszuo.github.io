About
I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Department of Computer Science & Engineering of The Chinese University of Hong Kong, hosted by Prof. Qiang Xu. I received my Ph.D. degree from The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) in 2026, advised by Prof. Yuzhe Ma.
My research sits at the intersection of electronic design automation and machine learning, and runs in both directions — using AI to design better chips, and designing better chips for AI.
  • Machine learning for electronic design automation
  • LLM agents for chip design automation
  • Emerging architectures for efficient LLM inference
